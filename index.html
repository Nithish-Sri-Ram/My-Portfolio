              Voxity

          

          
A frontier robotics company. We build the worlds machines are trained in, and the intelligence they run on.

          
        


        

          

            

              A robot in the field meets clutter, uneven ground and work nobody rehearsed. That's the condition we
              design for, not the edge case. Voxity starts with the layer robotics is missing: simulation,
              the worlds a machine practises in before the real one.
            

            voxity.org ↗

              SimFabric

          

          
The robotics training platform. Your robot needs somewhere to practise: describe it in a sentence.

    1. 
              
      01 · Build the world
      
              
      A week of scene building, in a sentence.
      
              
      No hand-placing meshes. Every object comes with its collision shape, mass and friction, and the world is checked in physics before you see it.
      
            
      2. 
            
    2. 
              
      02 · Train in worlds
      
              
      Train it where you already train.
      
              
      Every world downloads as SDFormat, MJCF, OpenUSD and a ROS 2 package. Gazebo, MuJoCo, or anything that reads USD.
      
            
      3. 
            
    3. 
              
      03 · Learn and adapt
      
              
      See. Act. Adapt. Repeat.
      
              
      The same aisle with the boxes moved, a slicker floor, a steeper ramp. Each variant is a new world, so the real world isn't its first try.

              Earlier work

    01 · Flutter · Speech · Summaries
    
                
    Smart Meet AI
    
                
    A multilingual meeting minutes and summary generator, built in Flutter.
    
                
    
                  It transcribes meetings accurately and turns them into minutes automatically, with speaker
                  identification and improved transcription accuracy. Users get accurate, real-time summaries in
                  multiple languages, without anyone taking notes. It served a large user base.
                
    
                
    
                  
                
    
              
    
            
    

            
    
              
    
                
    
                  
                
    
                
              
    
              
    
                
    02 · Python · FastAPI · Asterisk · FreePBX · Flutter
    
                
    Voice Bridge
    
                
    Live call transcription for deaf and mute users, built for Indian languages.
    
                
    
                  A mobile app that transcribes phone calls in real time, so a conversation can happen without
                  hearing it. I trained a custom model to support multiple regional languages and integrated it with
                  VoIP systems for live, accurate transcription. Voice-to-text and text-to-voice work both ways,
                  making calls accessible and inclusive.
                
    
                
    
                  Learn more ↗
                
    
              
    
            
    

            
    
              
    
                
    
                  
                
    
                
              
    
              
    
                
    03 · ESP8266 · Wi-Fi · IoT
    
                
    Steer Bot
    
                
    Drive an RC car from anywhere, with a real steering wheel and pedals.
    
                
    
                  An IoT steering control system: an ESP8266 microcontroller on Wi-Fi takes input from a racing
                  wheel and drives the car through the cloud, from any location. Smooth steering, precise throttle
                  and real-time response make it feel like driving a real vehicle. Hardware, made remote.
